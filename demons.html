Upper Demons

Muzan Kibutsuji

The progenitor and King of all demons, Muzan is the primary antagonist who seeks to become immune to sunlight and achieve true immortality

Kokushibo (Upper Rank One)

A former Demon Slayer and the highest-ranked Upper Moon, he wields a flesh katana and an advanced form of Moon Breathing techniques.

Doma (Upper Rank Two)

A seemingly friendly yet apathetic demon and leader of a cult, Doma uses powerful cryokinesis (ice manipulation) techniques in battle.

Akaza (Upper Rank Three)

A martial arts expert with a tragic human past, he values strength and specializes in rapid, destructive hand-to-hand combat using shockwaves.

Hantengu (Upper Rank Four)

A timid and cowardly demon whose Blood Demon Art allows him to split into multiple, powerful clones that each embody one of his emotions.

Gyokko (Upper Rank Five)

An eccentric demon obsessed with "art" who can manipulate water and use his grotesque, vase-like body to summon deadly fish creatures.

Gyutaro & Daki (Upper Rank Six)

A sibling duo who share the rank and must be decapitated simultaneously to be defeated; Gyutaro uses poisoned sickles, while Daki uses sharp cloth sashes.

Nakime ( 2nd Upper Rank Four)

Nakime was the Biwa Demon and the final Upper Rank Four of the Twelve Kizuki, whose Blood Demon Art allowed her complete manipulation over the architecture of the infinite, dimension-warping Infinity Castle.

kaigaku (2nd Upper Rank five)

Kaigaku is a former Demon Slayer and Upper Rank Six demon who betrayed his fellow slayers to become a demon, motivated by a desire for power and jealousy towards his fellow disciple, Zenitsu.

Nezuko Kamado

Nezuko is a unique demon who, despite being transformed by the Demon King Muzan, retains her humanity, refuses to harm humans, and eventually becomes the only demon with immunity to sunlight.

Lady Tamayo

Lady Tamayo is an ancient, benevolent demon and skilled doctor who, after being freed from Muzan's control, devoted centuries to researching a cure for demonism and ultimately aided the Demon Slayer Corps in his defeat.

Yushiro

Yushiro is a proud, short-tempered demon created by Tamayo who serves as her devoted assistant and bodyguard, using his "Blindfold" Blood Demon Art to manipulate vision and mask presences while providing crucial medical and tactical support to the Demon Slayer Corps against Muzan
